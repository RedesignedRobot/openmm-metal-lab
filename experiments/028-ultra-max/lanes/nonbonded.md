## Starting facts from earlier work

- 024 round 2 (M3 Ultra, before the event wait fix): HIP's `numTilesInBatch = numAtomBlocks < 2000 ? 4 : 1` lifted rf 0.88 to 1.01+ and pme to 1.07+ against `metal`. It was never committed. The gbsa idle gap it was fighting was fixed later by 6df2b8bcb, so it needs a fresh screen.
- 024 stage 2 knobs were tuned on the M2's 10 cores: 40 force blocks of 64 threads per core (2,400 blocks on 60 cores), 12 thread blocks per core, padding 0.08 (HIP), findBlocksWithInteractions at 32 threads per threadgroup.
- 025 screen 2 (M2): `metal`+P0 was 4 to 7 percent faster than 6df2b8bcb on pme, apoa1rf, apoa1pme and cellulose. P0's findBlocks body inside 6df2b8bcb gave nothing (screen 1, p0/scr 0.98 to 1.02), so the difference is elsewhere. The obvious candidate is the force kernel: `metal` stages block Y in threadgroup memory and accumulates Y forces there, 256-thread groups, 6 per core, no per-pair `r2 < cutoff` branch; HIP's kernel rotates position, charge, parameters, force and the atom index through `simd_shuffle_rotate_down` every j step.
