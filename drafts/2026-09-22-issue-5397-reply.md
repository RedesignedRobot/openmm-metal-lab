@NORPG, I built your Objective-C branch (8f6a7332) on both machines to see if I could help test it. It builds with just the Command Line Tools, no Xcode, and `TestMetalComputeContext` passes on the M2 and the M3 Ultra. Two things from my measurements that touch it, take or leave:
- `fastMathEnabled = YES` in `MetalContext.mm`: with fast math the results drift from OpenCL. `mathMode = MTLMathModeSafe` with precise math functions brought sqrt, rsqrt, divide and fma back to bit-identical.
- `MetalKernel::execute` takes a new command buffer per kernel. That cost me 15 to 24 µs per dispatch. One encoder for the whole step cost 0.11 µs, slightly under `clEnqueueNDRangeKernel`.
