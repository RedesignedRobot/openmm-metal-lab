Thanks @peastman, good to know the macOS 14 floor and the erfc gap are both non-issues. Sorry about the ticks bug, it cost me a day too.

@NORPG, I built your Objective-C branch (8f6a7332) on both machines to see if I could help test it. It builds with just the Command Line Tools, no Xcode, and TestMetalComputeContext passes on the M2 and the M3 Ultra.

Two things from my measurements:

1. fastMathEnabled = YES in MetalContext.mm: with fast math the results drift from OpenCL. mathMode = MTLMathModeSafe with precise math functions brought sqrt, rsqrt, divide and fma back to bit-identical.

2. MetalKernel::execute takes a new command buffer per kernel. That cost me 15 to 24 microseconds per dispatch. One encoder for the whole step cost 0.11 microseconds, slightly under clEnqueueNDRangeKernel.
