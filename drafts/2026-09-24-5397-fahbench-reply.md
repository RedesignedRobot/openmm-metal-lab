@peastman It's the same OpenMM on both sides. I run the FAHBench work units on current OpenMM through a small script, not the FAHBench app. The difference is constraints. FAHBench's dhfr constrains every bond, so CCMA handles 3,072 constraints, while benchmark.py's dhfr uses HBonds and sends nothing to CCMA. When the 1,302 heavy-atom constraints are turned into bonds, Metal and OpenCL tie on the M3 Ultra (220.7 vs 215.3 ns/day). So OpenCL loses more time to CCMA than Metal does. Happy to test anything you put together on my M2, M3 Pro and M3 Ultra.
